# DairyEco AI — Phase 0 Dataset Audit Report

## 1. Executive Summary

This report documents the comprehensive Phase 0 audit of `data/raw/` for **DairyEco AI**, executed strictly under the non-negotiable research rules of `vision.md` (Revision 2.2). All `8,096` raw files (`245,170,139` bytes) were recursively inventoried, hashed (`SHA-256`), and inspected without modifying any file or directory under `data/raw/`.

### Key Audit Takeaways
1. **All 5 required sources are present and verified** (`4` with `high` confidence from internal titles/XML/JSON-LD metadata; `pdo_cheese_lca` with `medium` confidence because internal Excel metadata lacks an explicit DOI/author/licence block, although its 44 French PDO cheese tables and INAO URLs are unambiguous).
2. **AGRIBALYSE 3.2 (`data/raw/agribalyse_3_2/`)** provides `2,458` food product rows (`FU = 1 kg of consumed food product`, EF 3.1, France) including **`255` core dairy product records** (`223` in *lait et produits laitiers*, `10` in *beurres*, `22` in *glaces/desserts glacés*), plus `19` infant/beverage dairy rows and **`14` farm-gate milk/dairy animal systems** (`FU = 1 kg leaving farm gate`) across conventional (`8`) and organic (`6`) agriculture workbooks. **Crucial finding:** Product-level totals in `Synthese` already include baseline packaging (`PACK AGB` or `PACK PROXY`), which is isolated as the `Emballage` stage in `Detail etape`.
3. **French PDO Cheese LCA (`data/raw/pdo_cheese_lca/`)** represents **`44` unique French PDO cheeses** (`FU = 1 kg of ripened cheese` at ripening exit, excluding packaging, retail, and consumer stages). Its `data_PDOcheeses_LCIA.xlsx` table contains **`1,232` non-empty rows (`44 cheeses × 28 EF 3.0/ILCD indicators`)** across two ripening scenarios (`Base` at factory vs. `Alternative` in a shared ripening room). These `1,232` rows are long-format indicator breakdowns of `44` cheeses and **must never be treated as `1,232` independent machine-learning observations**.
4. **USLCI (`data/raw/uslci/`)** is an openLCA v2.0.3 JSON-LD v3 export (`1,434` processes, `5,708` flows) with **zero bundled LCIA methods (`0` impact method files)** and **`97` Bridge Processes** (`USLCI to US Electricity Baseline` and `USLCI to USEEIO`) that contain `0` elementary flows. Consequently, standalone `UNIT_PROCESS` records in `data/raw/uslci/` cannot be rolled up into cradle-to-gate GWP100 impacts without either linking external Federal LCA Commons repositories (`US Electricity Baseline`, `USEEIO`) + TRACI 2.1/IPCC characterization factors, or using the NIST/ANL 2024 pre-characterized regional polymer carbon intensities (`data/reference/nist_plastic_carbon_intensity.csv`). Furthermore, **container glass (bottles/jars) and liquid packaging board (LPB / aseptic cartons) are absent from USLCI**.
5. **NIST/ANL (`ANL/ESIA-24/3`, 2024) and IFC/World Bank (2007) PDFs** have been extracted into verified machine-readable CSV tables under `data/interim/pdf_extraction/` and `data/reference/`, checked cell-by-cell against 150-DPI and 250-DPI rendered PDF pages.

### 3.3 Packaging Inclusion and Stage Contribution in Core Dairy (`N = 255`)
**Yes — packaging is included in all AGRIBALYSE 3.2 food product totals.**
- Across the `255` core dairy products, `Approche emballage` is `'PACK AGB'` (category-representative packaging market mix from Planet Score / ADEME data) for `230` products and `'PACK PROXY'` (proxy packaging extrapolated from similar subcategories) for `25` products.
- In `Detail etape`, for Climate Change (`Changement climatique`, `kg CO2 eq / kg`):
  - **`Agriculture` stage:** `0.818` – `14.544 kg CO2 eq/kg` (median `4.365 kg CO2 eq/kg`, accounting for ~`75%–90%` of total dairy GWP).
  - **`Transformation` stage:** `0.000` (`"-"`) – `1.701 kg CO2 eq/kg` (median `0.388 kg CO2 eq/kg`).
  - **`Emballage` (Packaging) stage:** `0.075` – `0.785 kg CO2 eq/kg` (median `0.214 kg CO2 eq/kg`, mean `0.228 kg CO2 eq/kg`, representing `1.0%` to `18.7%` of total product GWP depending on format and milk intensity).
  - **`Transport` stage:** `0.159` – `0.483 kg CO2 eq/kg` (median `0.218 kg CO2 eq/kg`).
  - **`Supermarché et distribution` stage:** `0.017` – `0.119 kg CO2 eq/kg` (median `0.036 kg CO2 eq/kg`).
  - **`Consommation` stage:** `0.000` (`"-"`) – `0.130 kg CO2 eq/kg` (median `0.007 kg CO2 eq/kg`).
- **Implication for DairyEco AI:** When replacing a dairy product's packaging in Phase 1+, we must subtract the baseline `Emballage` stage value (`Detail etape`) from the `Synthese` total (or sum the 5 non-packaging stages) before adding the alternative packaging system's cradle-to-grave impact, to prevent double-counting packaging.

### 4.2 Join Keys and Required Normalization (`normalize_pdo_cheese_name`)
The true observation entity across the 7 cheese-level tables is the **PDO cheese (`N = 44`)**. However, raw string joins fail unless normalized via `dairyeco.data.audit_inspectors.normalize_pdo_cheese_name()`:
1. **Trailing whitespace:** Almost every cheese name in `characterization` and `LCIA` has 1–2 trailing spaces (`'Beaufort '`, `"Bleu d'Auvergne  "`), and 9 cheeses carry trailing spaces in all 7 tables.
2. **Spelling discrepancy (`Camenbert` vs `Camembert`):** Row index 9 is spelled `'Camenbert de Normandie '` (with an `n`) in `characterization` and `LCIA`, whereas it is spelled `'Camembert de Normandie '` (with an `m`) in `allocations`, `coproducts`, `distances`, `specifications`, and `LCI`.
3. **Verification:** Applying `name.strip()` and replacing `'Camenbert de Normandie'` with `'Camembert de Normandie'` yields **exact 44-to-44 set equality across all 7 cheese-level tables and both LCI scenarios**.

### 4.3 Functional Unit, Boundaries, and Climate Impact Summary (`N = 44`)
- **Functional Unit:** `1 kg of ripened cheese` (`FU = 1kg of cheese`).
- **Boundaries:** Covers `Milk production`, `Milk transport`, `Milk pumping`, `Milk storage`, `Milk processing`, `Cheese ripening`, and `Equipment cleaning` (plus `Cheese transport` in the `Alternative` shared-ripening scenario). **Packaging, retail distribution, and household consumption are excluded.**
- **Climate Change (`kg CO2 eq / kg ripened cheese`) across the 44 cheeses (`data_PDOcheeses_LCIA.xlsx`):**
  - *Base scenario (`Ripening at cheese factory`):* Mean `6.968 kg CO2 eq/kg`, median `6.906`, min `5.211` (*Bleu du Vercors-Sassenage*), max `12.642` (*Roquefort*). `Milk production` accounts for `85.9%–96.4%` of total GWP across all 44 cheeses (`mean 92.8%`).
  - *Alternative scenario (`Ripening in a shared ripening room`):* Mean `6.984 kg CO2 eq/kg`, median `6.921`, min `5.224`, max `12.659` (`+0.008` to `+0.036 kg CO2 eq/kg` higher than the Base scenario due to inter-site refrigerated transport and deeper cellar electricity/wood inputs).

## 5. USLCI Database Audit (`data/raw/uslci/`)

### 5.1 Database Architecture and Critical Limitations
- **Total Processes:** `1,434` (`1,408` `UNIT_PROCESS` + `26` `LCI_RESULT`).
- **No Bundled LCIA Methods (`lcia_method_count = 0`):** The raw openLCA JSON-LD export contains no `lcia_methods/` or `lcia_categories/` directory. Computing GWP100 or TRACI 2.1 midpoint scores directly from `data/raw/uslci/` requires mapping elementary flows (`Carbon dioxide, fossil`, `Methane, fossil`, `Dinitrogen monoxide`, etc.) to external IPCC AR5/AR6 or TRACI 2.1 characterization factors.
- **`97` Bridge Processes and Unlinked Electricity Supply Chain:**
  - `27` processes in `22: Utilities / 2211: Electric Power Generation, Transmission and Distribution / Bridge Processes/USLCI to US Electricity Baseline` and `55` processes in `Bridge Processes/USLCI to USEEIO` (plus `15` USFS/equipment bridge stubs) contain **zero elementary flows** (`2` product exchanges only, with `defaultProvider = None` on the input side).
  - Overall, across all `1,434` processes, **`2,171` of `6,833` product/waste input exchanges lack a `defaultProvider`** inside `data/raw/uslci/`.
  - **Consequence:** Any `UNIT_PROCESS` in `data/raw/uslci/` that consumes grid electricity (`Electricity, at Grid, US`, WECC, SERC, RFC, MRO, NPCC, ERCOT, etc.) has its upstream electricity emissions truncated to zero unless paired with external grid emission factors (such as the NIST/ANL 2024 grid factors `400 gCO2e/kWh` for USA or eGRID/US Electricity Baseline).

### Recommended Phase 1 Harmonization Architecture
1. **Baseline Dairy Product Footprint (`DairyEco AI` Stage 1–2):** Use `AGRIBALYSE 3.2` `Detail etape` (`255` core dairy products) with the **`Emballage` stage subtracted** to obtain a clean, packaging-free baseline product footprint across `Agriculture`, `Transformation`, `Transport`, `Supermarché et distribution`, and `Consommation`. Use `pdo_cheese_lca` (`44` cheeses) to validate processing vs. milk-production sensitivity and co-product allocation rules.
2. **Packaging Material Carbon Intensity (`DairyEco AI` Stage 3):**
   - For **virgin polymers (`PET`, `HDPE`, `LDPE`, `PP`, `PVC`)**, use the extracted **NIST/ANL 2024 regionalized cradle-to-gate carbon intensities** (`data/reference/nist_plastic_carbon_intensity.csv`) as the primary GWP100 reference across `Western Europe`, `USA`, `China`, `MENA`, and `Global average`.
   - For **mechanically recycled polymers (`rPET`, `rHDPE`, `rPP`), plastic converting (`injection molding`, `thermoforming`, `film extrusion`), corrugated board, aluminum, transport, and EoL**, combine the curated **USLCI unit processes** (normalized to `1 kg` or `1 t*km`) with explicit IPCC GWP100 characterization factors and regional grid electricity emission factors (`300–641 gCO2e/kWh` from NIST/ANL Table 4 or user-selected Uzbekistan grid factor).
   - For **missing USLCI materials (container glass, liquid packaging board / aseptic cartons)**, either back-calculate reference stage intensities from `AGRIBALYSE 3.2` glass/carton dairy products or ingest an explicit open published reference table with `source_type = "published_reference"` / `"proxy"`.
3. **Packaging Mass & Factory Utility Plausibility Bounds:** Use **IFC/World Bank 2007 Table 3** (`data/reference/ifc_dairy_benchmarks.csv`) (`3.5–35 g packaging / kg milk`, `8–30 g / kg cheese`, `8–29 g / kg milk powder`) to constrain and validate packaging-to-product mass ratios (`g packaging / kg dairy product`).
