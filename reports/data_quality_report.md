# DairyEco AI — Phase 0 Data Quality Report

## 1. Purpose

This report catalogues all data quality anomalies, structural pitfalls, schema/unit inconsistencies, missing metadata, and cross-dataset incompatibilities discovered during the Phase 0 audit of `data/raw/`. Every issue listed below has been verified directly from the raw files and must be guarded against in Phase 1 data ingestion pipelines.

## 3. Cross-Dataset Incompatibilities to Prevent Blind Merging

1. **System Boundary Mismatch (`Cradle-to-Grave` vs `Cradle-to-Ripening-Exit` vs `Cradle-to-Gate`):**
   - `AGRIBALYSE 3.2` food rows are cradle-to-grave (`1 kg consumed at consumer`, **including** baseline packaging, retail, and consumer stages).
   - `PDO Cheese LCA` rows are cradle-to-ripening-exit (`1 kg ripened cheese`, **excluding** packaging, retail, and consumer stages).
   - `NIST/ANL 2024` polymer values are cradle-to-gate (`1 kg virgin resin`, **excluding** converting into bottles/cups, transport, and end-of-life).
   - `USLCI` processes are gate-to-gate unit processes (`UNIT_PROCESS`) unless rolled up.
2. **LCIA Method & Climate Indicator Differences:**
   - `AGRIBALYSE 3.2` uses **`EF 3.1 (adapted) v1.00`** (`Changement climatique` = sum of fossil, biogenic, and LULUC).
   - `PDO Cheese LCA` uses **`EF 3.0 / ILCD Midpoint`**.
   - `NIST/ANL 2024` uses **`GREET 2023 GWP100`**.
   - `USLCI` has **no bundled LCIA method** (`0` methods).
3. **Geography & Electricity Grid Mix Differences:**
   - `AGRIBALYSE 3.2` and `PDO Cheese LCA` reflect the **French low-carbon nuclear-heavy electricity mix** (`~50–80 gCO2e/kWh`), which makes dairy processing (`Transformation`) and French cheese ripening appear significantly lower in electricity-related GHG emissions than in fossil-heavy grids (e.g., China `641 gCO2e/kWh`, MENA `616 gCO2e/kWh`, USA `400 gCO2e/kWh`, or Uzbekistan natural-gas-dominated grid).
4. **Statistical Non-Independence of Tabular Rows:**
   - `data_PDOcheeses_LCIA.xlsx` (`1,232` rows) = `44` cheeses × `28` indicators.
   - `nist_plastic_carbon_intensity.csv` (`67` rows) = `5` polymers × regional/pathway deterministic LCA models.
   - `ifc_dairy_benchmarks.csv` (`69` rows) = published European/Nordic engineering benchmark ranges.
   - None of these tables represent independent factory-level random samples suitable for naïve row-wise supervised ML training without respecting physical grouping and deterministic LCA equations.
